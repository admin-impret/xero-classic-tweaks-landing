Privacy policy

Classic Tweaks for Xero

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The extension collects nothing

The Classic Tweaks for Xero browser extension does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. Your settings (which tweaks are switched on) are saved locally in your own browser using chrome.storage and never leave your device. The extension has no analytics, no tracking, and no external servers of its own.

Buying a license

If you buy a license, payment is processed by Paddle, our payment provider and merchant of record. Paddle collects the information needed to process your payment (such as your email address and payment details) under Paddle's own privacy policy. We receive only your email address and a transaction reference, which we use to send you your license key and to verify it later.

License verification

When you activate a license key, the extension sends the key (and nothing else) to our license server to check it is valid.
